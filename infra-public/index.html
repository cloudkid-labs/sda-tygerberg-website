Application infrastructure

Tygerberg SDA

The bilingual Astro site is served by a Cloudflare Worker. The Worker also handles the site’s live status, forms, member pages and mail workflows.

Request and data path

Source-derived overview · no production data access
01 · VISITOR

Browser

Loads Afrikaans or English public pages, contacts the church, or opens a member workflow.

02 · EDGE

Cloudflare Worker

Serves Astro assets and routes live-status, Turnstile-checked forms, CMS, sign-in and mail requests.

03 · STATE

R2 and Worker bindings

R2 stores CMS pages, form submissions, consent records and the church-controlled mail archive.

04 · SERVICES

YouTube, Brevo and email

YouTube supplies live status; Brevo handles newsletter delivery; Cloudflare Email Routing forwards mail and archives copies.

Security and boundaries

  • Secrets stay in Cloudflare Worker configuration; this page includes no values.
  • Turnstile protects contact submissions when configured.
  • CMS and member operations stay behind the Worker’s sign-in and access checks.
